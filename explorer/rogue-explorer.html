How a round works

  1. Two of the five agents total the same line item and each posts an answer and a one-line note.
  2. If they disagree, the ledger records the average, so a wrong answer moves the entry by half its error.
  3. The monitor sees who disagreed with whom, never the true total, and may quarantine one agent per round.
  4. Each game is scored on whether and when the rogue was caught, how many honest agents were removed, and how far the ledger ended up off.

The baseline monitor strikes both agents in every disagreement and removes anyone with two strikes.
